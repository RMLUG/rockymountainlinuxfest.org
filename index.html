About Us

We're bringing together Linux enthusiasts, open-source developers, and technology professionals for a conference focused on learning, collaboration, and the open-source community. Whether you're a seasoned sysadmin, a developer, a longtime Linux user, or simply curious about open source, Rocky Mountain Linux Fest is a place to connect with others and discover something new.

Our conference brings people together through talks, hands-on workshops, demonstrations, and conversations covering Linux, open source software, development, system administration, and the technologies shaping our community.
