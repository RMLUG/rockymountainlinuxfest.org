About Us

Rocky Mountain Linux Fest is a free, community-driven event celebrating Linux, open source, and the people who make it all possible.

Join us in Denver for a day of learning, sharing, connecting, and exploring open-source technology! Whether you’re a longtime Linux enthusiast or just getting started, this event will leave you curious for more!
